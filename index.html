How to learn

Three steps to reading the Quran

1Read with a teacher

Read every chapter together with a qualified teacher. The explanations in the book are only there to support this.

2Practise in the book

Do the exercises and homework directly in the online book or on the printed PDF.

3Reinforce with the games

Revise letters and vowels with “Harf by Harf” – and discover the Seerah with Fuffl.

!

Tip from the book: Don't write English letters next to the Arabic words. Experience shows that people who do this learn twice as slowly.

Questions & answers

Good to know

I'm a teacher – can I use this with my class?

Yes, that is exactly what it is for. Use the book on the board or let students read it online, print the worksheets, posters and certificates for your class, and use the games in lessons or as homework. Everything is free – please just don't sell it. Questions? Write to us.

What age is it suitable for?

The games and colouring sheets are made for children aged about 8 to 14. Younger children can join in with help from their parents; the book itself is suitable for any age.

Do I need a teacher?

Yes, the book recommends it too: correct pronunciation is best learnt with a qualified teacher. The book, games and sheets help with practice at home.

Does it cost anything?

No. The book is a free e-book from Abrar, and all games and printables are free too. They may not be sold.

Does it work on phones and tablets?

Yes. The online book and both games adapt to phone, tablet and computer. In the book you can write with your finger and zoom.

Are our notes and game progress saved?

Yes, in the browser of the device you use. On another device you start afresh.

How do I best get started?

Print the learning plan: for 12 weeks it shows which book pages, game lessons and colouring sheets are next.
